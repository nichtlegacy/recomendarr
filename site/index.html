Get your picks

No sign-up and no app. Your Letterboxd username is all it takes.

  1. Comment your usernameIn the Reddit thread, e.g. nichtlegacy. Add a release span like 1990-2010 if you only want those years. Prefer private? Send a DM.
  2. Get your linkA reply links to your page: ranked picks, posters, Letterboxd links and why each film fits.
  3. Import the listOne CSV, imported as a Letterboxd list in ranked order, with the reason in each entry's notes.

Your data

Only what's already public on Letterboxd, and only for people who asked.

  • Only on requestA page exists only because someone asked for it. There's no directory of users.
  • Link onlyPages sit at unguessable addresses and tell search engines to stay away.
  • Public data onlyOnly your public diary and ratings are read. Your page shows picks, not your diary.
  • Removed on requestAsk on Reddit or open an issue, and the page is deleted. Pages are stored privately, never in the public repository.
